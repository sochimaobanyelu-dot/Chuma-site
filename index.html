A few useful answers

Before you book.

A little more about how Chuma Benz approaches Mercedes-Benz servicing and repairs.

Do you work on all Mercedes-Benz models?

We focus on Mercedes-Benz vehicles. Include your model and year in your request so we can confirm the service you need.

Can I ask about a warning light before booking?

Yes. Describe the warning and any symptoms in your request. We will follow up to discuss a suitable diagnostic check.

Will repairs start before I approve them?

Recommended work is discussed with you first. We seek your approval before proceeding with additional repairs.

How do I arrange a workshop visit?

Send a request through the form above with your Mercedes model, contact number and a short description. The workshop will follow up to arrange a suitable time.
